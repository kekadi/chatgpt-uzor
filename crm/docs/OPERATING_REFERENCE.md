# Operating reference — proposed, awaiting practice approval

Owner: Practice Administrator | Clinical approver: Medical Director | Version 0.1 | Reviewed 2026-10-12

## Escalation matrix

The demo's thresholds are elapsed-hour coordination alerts, not clinical triage standards: STAT yellow 1/red 2; Urgent yellow 12/red 24; Routine yellow 72/red 120. A STAT request requires immediate clinician-directed escalation and an acknowledged handoff; staff must not wait for the software threshold. Unexpected symptoms, deterioration or inability to arrange safe care follow the practice's clinical emergency instructions.

Owners review open queues at opening, midday and close. Supervisors resolve missing ownership and coverage. The Medical Director approves urgency definitions, clock-start events, business-hours/holiday handling, pauses and closure requirements before activation. Threshold changes require a version and approval date.

## Referral job aid

Validate identity, order and urgency; check coverage/network and authorization; record receiving specialist and transmission evidence; confirm appointment/access barriers; obtain consultation findings; route to the responsible clinician; document reviewed follow-up and close. A transmission receipt or scheduled appointment alone does not establish closed-loop completion. Route cancellation, refusal and inability to contact through distinct disposition reasons.

## DME job aid

Record clinician request, equipment/quantity, vendor and coverage context. Confirm current applicable order, supporting documentation, face-to-face, written-order-prior-to-delivery and prior-authorization requirements for the specific item. Coordinate documentation with the supplier without inventing clinical findings. Track acceptance, delivery and unresolved barriers. Exact requirements depend on the item and policy; a generic checklist is not evidence of coverage.

Primary references: [CMS DMEPOS order and face-to-face requirements](https://www.cms.gov/data-research/monitoring-programs/medicare-fee-service-compliance-programs/medical-review-and-education/dmepos-order-requirements); [CMS DMEPOS prior authorization](https://www.cms.gov/data-research/monitoring-programs/medicare-fee-service-compliance-programs/prior-authorization-and-pre-claim-review-initiatives/prior-authorization-process-certain-durable-medical-equipment-prosthetics-orthotics-and-supplies).

## PA, denial and P2P job aid

Verify payer, requested service and documentation; capture submission reference and follow-up deadline. Preserve the actual payer denial letter, code, narrative and date. Distinguish payer-specific authorization reasons from standardized claim adjustment/remittance codes; do not infer one from the other. Record appeal rights/deadlines from the applicable notice.

Prepare a concise case summary, order/indication, prior treatment and supporting findings for the reviewing clinician. Record P2P reviewer, appointment, decision, authorization/reference and next action. The CRM supports coordination; it does not independently supply medical necessity or make clinical decisions. Supervisors escalate an approaching appeal deadline to the clinician and administrator.

## KPI definitions and targets

Open cases: nonarchived cases without a close time. Overdue cases: open cases above the applicable approved red threshold. Completion percentage in the development workbook: completed cases divided by nonarchived cases in that module; this is not yet a cohort-based quality measure. Monthly opened counts use opened_at and completed counts use closed_at in America/Chicago, excluding the current partial month. Productivity is a current-owner snapshot until event-based reporting is implemented.

Suggested internal targets for review: all open cases have an owner and next action; all STAT handoffs acknowledged; all denials have the notice and deadline recorded; all completed referrals have documented closure evidence. Percentage and turnaround targets require baseline measurement before approval.

## Privacy and job-based access

Use approved systems and verified recipients; apply practice policies for job-based information access. Export only authorized information and distribute it through an approved channel. Do not place patient information in public repositories, support screenshots, ordinary email or unapproved OCR/AI services. Record authorized caregiver/representative relationships; an emergency contact does not automatically have authority over all records.

Primary reference: [HHS minimum necessary guidance](https://www.hhs.gov/hipaa/for-professionals/privacy/guidance/minimum-necessary-requirement/index.html). Apply the relevant exceptions and practice policy; minimum-necessary requirements do not apply identically to every treatment disclosure.
